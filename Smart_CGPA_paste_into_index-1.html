A realistic weekly study reset
  • Write down your next tests and assignment deadlines.
  • Start with the topics you find most difficult, in short focused sessions.
  • Practise questions from memory before checking notes.
  • Review every mistake and ask for help on topics you cannot explain.
  • Begin revision early and get enough rest.

How are GPA and CGPA calculated?

Semester GPA = total of (each course’s grade point × its credit units) ÷ total semester credit units.

Updated CGPA = (previous CGPA × previous completed units + current semester grade points) ÷ (previous completed units + current semester units).

Smart CGPA uses the grade points you set above. It does not know your university’s official rules unless you enter them correctly.

Important: Smart CGPA is a study tool, not an official academic record. Confirm your grading scale and credit units with your department before relying on the result.
