How does Smart CGPA calculate results?

Semester GPA = sum of (grade point × course credit units) ÷ total semester credit units.

Updated CGPA = (previous CGPA × previous completed units + this semester’s grade points) ÷ total completed units after this semester.

For an exact official result, confirm your university’s rules and use its full grade-point totals if available.

Smart CGPA is a study aid, not an official university record. Confirm grade boundaries and credit units with your institution.
